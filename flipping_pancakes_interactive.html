🥞 Flipping Pancakes

CSC 2400 · Decrease-and-Conquer Algorithms · Levitin, Section 4.5, Exercise 12

Arrange pancakes of different sizes with the smallest at the top and the largest at the bottom. Click a pancake to flip it and all the pancakes above it.

Click a pancake to reverse the order of that pancake and all pancakes above it.

Textbook algorithm: Find the largest pancake that is out of order. If it is not at the top, flip it and all the pancakes above it to bring it to the top. Then flip the pancakes above those already in their correct positions to put that pancake where it belongs. Repeat until all pancakes are in order. This algorithm does not necessarily use the fewest possible flips.
